# Feature & Engine Enhancement: Human-Technique Deduction Engine & Mathematical Difficulty Calibration

## Overview & Context
The current Undoku difficulty engine relies primarily on basic clue-carving heuristics and standard Singles deductions (Naked Singles, Hidden Singles in Box/Line). While functional, generating and grading high-tier puzzles (Hard/Expert, 17–25 clues) requires a deterministic, human-logic solver capable of evaluating subset reductions and candidate locking without falling back to brute force backtracking.

This ticket tasks the Antigravity CLI with extending the core solving engine, updating assertion calculus models, computing comprehensive trajectory metrics, and wiring the polar graph rendering.

---

## Technical Specifications & Requirements

### 1. Engine Core: Implement Advanced Deduction Strategies
Extend the logical solver pipeline (`engine/solver.ts` or equivalent) to evaluate human solving techniques in deterministic order:
1. **Naked Single** ($A_i \in [2, 22]$, $\text{Score} \in [1.06, 1.75]$)
2. **Hidden Single (Box)** ($A_i \in [10, 24]$, $\text{Score} \in [1.58, 1.85]$)
3. **Hidden Single (Line)** ($A_i \in [15, 32]$, $\text{Score} \in [1.98, 2.25]$)
4. **Locked Candidates (Pointing / Claiming)**
   - Assertion Model: $A_{\text{Locked}} = \max(18, \min(36, \text{round}(14 + 1.2 \cdot \text{Eliminations} + 0.5 \cdot \text{PeerCells})))$
   - Step Score: $\text{StepScore}_{\text{Locked}} = 2.40 + (A_{\text{Locked}} / 36.0) \times 0.60$
5. **Naked Pairs & Triples**
   - Assertion Model: $A_{\text{NakedSub}} = \text{round}(16 \cdot k + 2.5 \cdot \text{Eliminations} + 0.8 \cdot \text{OpenCells})$ for subset size $k \in \{2, 3\}$
   - Step Score: $\text{StepScore}_{\text{NakedSub}} = 2.80 + 0.50(k - 1) + (A_{\text{NakedSub}} / 48.0) \times 0.70$
6. **Hidden Pairs & Triples**
   - Assertion Model: $A_{\text{HiddenSub}} = \text{round}(22 \cdot k + 3.0 \cdot \text{Eliminations} + 1.0 \cdot \text{OpenCells})$
   - Step Score: $\text{StepScore}_{\text{HiddenSub}} = 3.50 + 0.60(k - 1) + (A_{\text{HiddenSub}} / 56.0) \times 0.80$

### 2. Analytical Metric Aggregator
Implement the calculation pipeline for the 28 standardized metrics categorized across the 7 domains:
- **Complexity & Assertions**: Total assertions ($A_{\text{total}}$), Peak step ($A_{\max}$), Mean proof burden ($\bar{A}$), Assertion density, and Composite workload index ($0.4 A_{\text{total}} + 3.5 \bar{A} + 0.8 A_{\max}$).
- **Difficulty & Scoring**: Step sum ($\sum S_i$), Composite cognitive score ($0.60 \cdot \text{Total} + 0.08 \cdot A_{\text{total}} + 1.50 \cdot \bar{A} + 2.00 \cdot \sigma^2$), Canonical Tier, and 8-tier Granular classification.
- **Statistical Trajectory**: $\mu, \sigma^2, \sigma, \text{MAD}, J_{\max}, i_{\text{bottleneck}}, \beta$ (OLS slope), and Pacing classification (*Balanced*, *Escalating*, *Front-Loaded*, *Volatile*).
- **Candidate Search & Entropy**: Unresolved candidate tracking, constrainedness index ($1.0 - \text{Clues} / 81$).
- **Topology & Geometry**: Symmetry assessment (180°, horizontal, vertical, diagonal), clue dispersion variance, box/band congestion.
- **Technique Composition**: Technique counts, Shannon diversity index ($-\sum p_t \ln p_t / \ln 6$), longest single-technique streak.
- **Constraint Matrix**: Row, column, and box peer elimination counts.

### 3. Granular Calibration Mapping
Ensure the generator validates generated puzzles against the exact tier requirements:
- **Easy (Tier 1 & 2)**: 36–41 clues | Score $< 50.0$ | Peak Assertions $\le 12$
- **Medium (Tier 1 & 2)**: 29–34 clues | Score $50.0 - 61.9$ | Peak Assertions $10 - 16$
- **Hard (Tier 1 & 2)**: 24–28 clues | Score $62.0 - 73.9$ | Peak Assertions $14 - 20$
- **Expert (Tier 1 & 2)**: 17–23 clues | Score $\ge 74.0$ | Peak Assertions $\ge 18$

### 4. Polar Graph Visualization Renderer
Implement canvas/rendering utility for the Polar Spike graph:
- **Radial Coordinate Computation**:
  $$\theta_i = -\frac{\pi}{2} + \left(\frac{i}{N}\right) \cdot 2\pi$$
  $$\text{Intensity}_i = \max\left(0.08, \min\left(1.00, 0.55 \cdot \frac{S_i - 1.00}{1.35} + 0.45 \cdot \frac{A_i - 2}{22}\right)\right)$$
  $$r_i = r_{\text{hub}} + \text{Intensity}_i \cdot (r_{\max} - r_{\text{hub}})$$
- **Visual Polish**:
  - Draw dynamic spike vectors with thickness $w_i \in [1.2, 4.0]\text{px}$ based on assertion load.
  - Implement Catmull-Rom or cubic spline interpolation connecting radial endpoints $(x_i, y_i)$ to generate a smooth cognitive perimeter contour.
  - Map stroke/fill colors dynamically using HSL interpolation: $\text{Hue} = 120^\circ \cdot (1.0 - \text{Intensity}_i)$.
